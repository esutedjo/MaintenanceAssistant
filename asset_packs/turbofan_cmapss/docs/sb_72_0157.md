SYNTHETIC DOCUMENT written for the hackathon demo. Not an OEM service bulletin.

## 1.A Reason
<!-- {components: [fan], failure_modes: [fan_degradation]} -->
Fan blade leading-edge erosion combined with abradable liner rub reduces fan efficiency. In trend data the
bypass ratio (BPR) falls while P30, fuel-flow ratio (phi) and coolant bleeds W31 and W32 rise.

## 3.B Recommended action
<!-- {components: [fan], failure_modes: [fan_degradation], tasks: [FM-FAN-01, FM-FAN-03], topic: fan_inspection_interval} -->
For engines with falling BPR, inspect fan blades and the abradable liner every 100 cycles (FM-FAN-01). When
an engine is removed for fan blade set replacement (FM-FAN-03), replace the fan case abradable liner
(TF-FAN-2103) at the same visit. Compliance: recommended.
