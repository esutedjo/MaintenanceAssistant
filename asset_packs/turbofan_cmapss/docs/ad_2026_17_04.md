## (c) Unsafe condition
<!-- {components: [hpc], failure_modes: [hpc_degradation]} -->
Erosion of HPC stage 1 to 3 blades beyond limits can lead to blade release and uncontained engine failure.

## (g) Required actions
<!-- {components: [hpc], failure_modes: [hpc_degradation], tasks: [FM-HPC-01], mandate: {failure_modes: [hpc_degradation], min_probability: 0.5, rul_lower_below: 30, task: FM-HPC-01, within_cycles: 10, action: "Borescope HPC stages 1-3 within 10 cycles; remove the engine if any blade is beyond limits"}} -->
For any engine where HPC degradation is indicated and the predicted remaining life is below 30 cycles,
borescope HPC stages 1 to 3 within 10 cycles (FM-HPC-01). If any blade is beyond the limits of AMM 72-31-00,
remove the engine before further flight. This action is mandatory and may not be replaced by an engine swap
alone: the removed engine must still be inspected before it is returned to service.
