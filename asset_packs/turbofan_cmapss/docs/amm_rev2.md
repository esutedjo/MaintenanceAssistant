## 72-31-00 HPC borescope inspection
<!-- {components: [hpc], failure_modes: [hpc_degradation], tasks: [FM-HPC-01], topic: hpc_borescope_interval} -->
Borescope HPC stages 1 to 9 every 90 cycles. No reduced interval is defined in this revision.

## 72-31-00-601 HPC blade borescope limits (table 601)
<!-- {components: [hpc], topic: "limits:borescope:hpc_blades"} -->
SYNTHETIC LIMITS, superseded by revision 3 (which tightened them); kept to show that a finding is compared only with
the revision in effect.
Limit HPC-TIP: blade tip erosion, mm; serviceable up to 0.60; reject above 0.90.
Limit HPC-NICK: leading-edge nick depth, mm; serviceable up to 0.30; reject above 0.60.
Interval: within limits next inspection 90 cycles; at limit re-inspect within 45 cycles; beyond limits remove before
further flight.
