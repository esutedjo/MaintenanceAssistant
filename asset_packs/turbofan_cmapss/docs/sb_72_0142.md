SYNTHETIC DOCUMENT written for the hackathon demo. Not an OEM service bulletin.

## 1.A Reason
<!-- {components: [hpc], failure_modes: [hpc_degradation]} -->
Fleet reports show faster than expected leading-edge erosion of HPC stage 1 to 3 blades in sandy and hot
operating environments, visible in trend data as rising T30 and Ps30 with falling P30 at constant thrust.

## 3.B Recommended action
<!-- {components: [hpc], failure_modes: [hpc_degradation], tasks: [FM-HPC-01, FM-HPC-03], topic: hpc_borescope_interval} -->
For engines whose trend monitoring indicates HPC degradation, reduce the HPC borescope interval to 25 cycles
(replaces the 30-cycle reduced interval in AMM 72-31-00). Use plug kit TF-GEN-8101. Compliance: recommended.
